# Hi there, I'm a 13-year-old Maker!

I started exploring hardware and software at 10. My ultimate goal is to become a **Hardware/Software Engineer & Cybersecurity Researcher**. I am deeply passionate about IoT, cryptography, and bringing innovative ideas to life. As a curious tinkerer, I love discovering how things work and finding vulnerabilities. My golden rule is always: **Break it to make it safer, never to cause harm.** 🛡️

## 🚀 My Journey
My passion for technology started at age 3 with robotics building blocks. Over the years, I transitioned through various robotics programs and even prepared for certifications. However, I eventually realized that standardized certifications couldn't satisfy my true curiosity. 

In the summer of 2023, I received an Arduino kit and made a bold decision: **I quit the certification path to dive into real open-source hardware.** I started with Arduino and Mixly, using visual programming to rigorously hone my logical thinking. Then came the 'aha' moment when I discovered Mixly could convert blocks into code. It was my very first encounter with C++, even though I didn't know its name back then! 

At the age of 12, my mother guided me into the world of OI (Olympiad in Informatics). My first CSP-J attempt was a classic OIer rite of passage: I barely passed the preliminary round, but got a brutal 0 points in the final round! I had only been studying OI for 3 months. During that contest, I wrote a solution for the first problem but had no idea if it was WA, TLE, or RE. I ended up with 40pts and completely gave up on the rest. It was a harsh but unforgettable reality check!

I bounced back for my second CSP-J attempt, scoring 20 points higher in the preliminary round and achieving 235 points in the final round to win the Second Prize. Although I missed the First Prize by a mere single-digit margin, that tiny gap only fueled my relentless drive to push my limits.

I then successfully completed a hardcore 20-day OI training camp hosted by Xinyou Team (the same organization that trained IOI Gold Medalist Kangyang Zhou). Competing in the Advanced Group completely reshaped my algorithmic mindset. I have now achieved the **'Green Name & Green Checkmark' (Intermediate Level)** on Luogu, marking a solid milestone in my algorithm journey!
